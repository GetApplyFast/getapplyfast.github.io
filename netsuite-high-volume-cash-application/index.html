High-volume NetSuite cash application without losing track of invoices

If you apply customer payments in NetSuite against dozens or hundreds of open invoices, the Accept Customer Payment screen gets slow and brittle. Checking invoices one by one makes it easy to lose track of what you already selected and applied. Moving to another page does not clear your selections. Changing a filter can drop a checked invoice only when that invoice no longer matches the filter, for example after narrowing by invoice date.

CSV import of Customer Payments works for some teams. AR and bank remittance platforms (HighRadius, Celigo Cash Application Manager, ZoneReconcile, and similar SuiteApps) work when you want a separate remittance-matching system. Many AR users still need to stay on Accept Customer Payment, paste a remittance from Excel, and apply the matches with a quick human review before they click Save in NetSuite.

ApplyFast is a Chrome extension built for that job.

What breaks at high invoice volume

  • Remittances that list many invoice or PO numbers against one payment
  • Manual find → tick Apply → enter amount → repeat
  • Losing track of which invoices you already selected and applied during manual line-by-line work
  • Checked invoices disappearing from the list when a filter narrows them out (for example invoice dates)
  • Batches that hang or feel unusable as the open-invoice list grows

What ApplyFast does on that screen

  1. Paste invoice / PO references from Excel (two columns: Ref | Amount, or three: Ref | Discount | Amount).
  2. Review every match and exception before anything is written.
  3. Apply matching lines on the current page or across pages (All pages scan, up to 20 pages).
  4. You check the totals and click Save in NetSuite yourself. ApplyFast never clicks Save.

Cash application, including multi-page apply, is free. Review & Reconciliation (exception analysis and Excel reconciliation export) is Premium. A 14-day Review & Reconciliation trial starts only after a clean successful Apply and only if you confirm it.

FAQ

Do I lose checked invoices when I change page?

No. Moving page to page does not clear your selections. A filter can remove a checked invoice only if that invoice no longer matches the filter, such as invoice dates.

Is ApplyFast a full cash application automation suite?

No. It is a lightweight helper inside Accept Customer Payment. It does not replace bank remittance ingest, lockbox AI, or SuiteApp-grade AR automation.

Does it work across multiple invoice pages?

Yes. All pages mode scans up to 20 pages, then previews and applies across the scanned pages. That capability is free.

Does ApplyFast send my NetSuite data anywhere?

Invoice and payment data stay in your browser. Only license checks go to Lemon Squeezy if you activate a license. See the Privacy Policy.
